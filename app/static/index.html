Navigation

Apple Maps follows its own fastest road between stops, so the stops below sit on the intended road and are close together. Tap each leg when you start it. If Apple Maps wanders, load the GPX in OsmAnd, which follows a track exactly.

Your WiGLE cell heat

Upload wigle_cell_heat.csv here, or copy it into the data folder on the Pi. It is stored on the Pi and used on every device. Replace it every 60 days (30 to 90 works).

Recent WiGLE density

Upload wigle_density.csv from wigle_density.py on your PC. It tells the planner how many WiFi networks WiGLE has seen recently in each cell, which is used to value cells nobody has recorded yet. Stored on the Pi. Without it, those cells are valued from nearby charted cells.

Add an area ahead of time

Type a city or address. The Pi builds the road data for 40 km around it, including cells nobody has recorded yet (10 to 25 minutes) and keeps it. Opening the page in a new place does this automatically from your location.

Assumptions behind the estimates

One pass through a cell is expected to find about pass fraction of the APs the holder has that you do not have yet. The defaults were calibrated against one real drive's before and after warroom data, where dense cells gained 4 to 30 percent of the holder count. Tune these if reality differs.

New BLE uses the 2.6 BLE per new WiFi seen in that same before and after data. XP counts 1 per new WiFi, 10 per capture, 2 per rescan, 0.2 per BLE. Whether the 10 replaces or adds to the 1 is unverified; this adds. Flip chance is the expected new WiFi divided by the gap to the holder, discounted for cells that flap between holders. Cells missing from your footprint are not shown.
